Cell Structure: The Fundamental Unit of Life

Understand prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, the plasma membrane and major organelles.

Cell: The Unit of LifecellorganellesNCERT

Every living organism is made up of one or more cells. The cell is the structural and functional unit of life.

Prokaryotic cells lack a membrane-bound nucleus, while eukaryotic cells possess a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.

The plasma membrane is selectively permeable. Mitochondria are involved in aerobic respiration, chloroplasts in photosynthesis, and ribosomes in protein synthesis.

Study tip: Draw and label a plant cell and an animal cell and compare their structural features.
