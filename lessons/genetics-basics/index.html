Mendelian Genetics: Laws of Inheritance

A beginner-friendly overview of dominance, segregation and independent assortment.

Principles of Inheritance and VariationgeneticsMendelinheritance

Gregor Mendel studied inheritance using pea plants. His experiments led to key principles explaining how traits pass between generations.

The law of segregation states that the two alleles of a gene separate during gamete formation.

The law of independent assortment describes the independent distribution of alleles of different genes when genes are unlinked. Linked genes may not assort independently.

Practice with monohybrid and dihybrid crosses to build confidence.
