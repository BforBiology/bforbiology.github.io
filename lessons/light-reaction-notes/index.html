CLASS 11 · NOTE

Light Reaction of Photosynthesis — Quick Notes

Discover Photosystem 2, the electron transport chain, ATP and NADPH formation.

Photosynthesis in Higher PlantsphotosynthesischloroplastATP

The light reaction takes place on the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts. Light energy excites electrons in the photosystems.

Photosystem 2 absorbs light and replaces lost electrons using electrons from water splitting, which releases oxygen and protons.

Electron transport builds a proton gradient that powers ATP synthase. Photosystem 1 energizes electrons again; NADP+ is reduced to NADPH.

ATP and NADPH support carbon fixation in the Calvin cycle. These are introductory notes: check the NCERT chapter for all details.
