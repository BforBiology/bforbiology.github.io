ABOUT US

Science explained clearly.

B for Biology provides free learning resources for Class 11 and Class 12 Biology learners, including NCERT-aligned notes, original diagrams, videos and quizzes.

Our approach

We aim to explain Biology in approachable Hindi and English with scientifically accurate terminology. The site is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with NCERT or a school examination board.

Editorial quality

Lessons are intended as study aids, and are reviewed for clarity before publication. Students should consult the current official syllabus and textbooks for exam requirements.
